Highlights

- Evaluates small-model code generation across HumanEval, MBPP, EvalPlus, and LiveCodeBench.
- Shows GPT-2 Medium does not reliably improve over GPT-2 Small for executable code.
- Compares GPT-2 with similarly sized CodeGen checkpoints and a fixed-scale CodeGen ladder.
- Uses layer ablation, graded intervention, error analysis, and sparse steering controls.
- Frames code-generation reliability as a product of model, prompt, decoding, and tests.
