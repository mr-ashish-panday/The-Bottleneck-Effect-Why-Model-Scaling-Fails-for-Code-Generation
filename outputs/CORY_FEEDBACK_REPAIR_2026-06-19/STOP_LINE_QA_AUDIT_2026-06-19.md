2. For MSA, already-franchised or mature franchisor-stage companies are RED.
3. Every qualified row must include a suggested phone/contact number or an explicit callable department route.
4. Web-form-only is never a clean pass unless the client explicitly accepts form routes.
5. Wrong decision-maker function is RED.
6. Stale, social-only, or indirect sources are at least YELLOW.
7. Revenue/scale qualifiers must have direct proof or an explicitly stated proxy.
8. If a caller cannot open the row and know who to ask for, it is not qualified.
9. Any new client correction becomes a permanent QA gate for every ICP, not only the file they complained about.
10. Send a smaller, cleaner list before a larger risky list.
