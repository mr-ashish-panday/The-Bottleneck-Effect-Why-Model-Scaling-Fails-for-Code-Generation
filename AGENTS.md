- Classify bottlenecks as `performance`, `learning`, `judgment`, `relationship`, or `recovery`.
- Concentrate resources on a few high-conviction bets.
- Delete low-leverage work aggressively.
- Make incentives explicit wherever they shape behavior.
- Communicate clearly and concisely.
- Judge outcomes, not process theater.
- Iterate fast while respecting hard constraints.
- Push closeout before expansion.
- Prefer artifacts, commits, screenshots, sent messages, drafts, or concrete deltas over self-report.
